# AUDIO DIRECTION: POKÉMON ANCIENT

## 1. Musical Identity & Instrumentation
- **Aesthetic**: Deep tribal percussion, primeval wooden flutes, brass fanfares, atmospheric wind drones, and resonant acoustic guitar.
- **Key Motifs**:
  - *Expedition Theme (Camp Ambervale)*: Hopeful, rhythmic, acoustic warmth with woodwinds.
  - *Primeval Wilds (Route 101/102)*: Gentle ambient jungle percussion and bird-like flute calls.
  - *Rival Encounter (Maya / Leo)*: Energetic, playful, romantic undertones transitioning into intense brass-driven competitive battle theme.
  - *Cataclysm Incursion*: Ominous low brass, metallic industrial percussion, distorted basslines.
