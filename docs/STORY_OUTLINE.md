# STORY OUTLINE: POKÉMON ANCIENT

## Act I: The Primordial Rift (Demo V1 Scope)
1. **Prologue - The Awakening**:
   - The player awakens inside the Field Tent at Camp Ambervale.
   - Professor Andrea Cycad welcomes the player as the newest research fellow stranded by the chronal fracture.
   - Sirens blare: Team Cataclysm forces have infiltrated the northern delta excavation (Site Alpha).
2. **The Awakening of the Starters**:
   - The player rushes to Site Alpha.
   - Three stabilized primordial capsules are presented: Frillsprout (Grass/Rock), Pyroraptor (Fire/Dark), Plesioling (Water/Dragon).
   - The player bonds with their starter and battles Cataclysm Grunt Jax.
3. **The Partner's Rivalry**:
   - Maya (if playing as Leo) or Leo (if playing as Maya) selects the counter-starter.
   - A friendly yet fiercely competitive battle takes place on the Route 103 river bluff, establishing their dynamic of mutual affection, shared survival, and tactical rivalry.
4. **The Expedition Dispatch**:
   - Professor Cycad awards the Research Pokédex and Running Shoes, dispatching the duo southward to map the ancient biosphere.

## Act II: The 8 Ancient Sanctuaries
- Traversing the ancient continent of Gondwana.
- Defeating the 8 Biome Wardens to gain sacred Keystone Emblems.
- Uncovering evidence that Team Cataclysm intends to trigger the "Second Impact" using geothermal drilling at Mount Obsidian.

## Act III: The Extinction Engine
- Infiltrating the Cataclysm Caldera Fortress.
- Double battle with Maya/Leo against Commander Vane and Director Kaelen.
- Aethelgard awakens to shield the continent from devastation.

## Postgame: The Chronal Horizon
- Unlocking the Cretaceous Wilds for high-level exploration, ancient titan hunts, and boss rematches.
