# ART DIRECTION: POKÉMON ANCIENT

## 1. Palette & Atmosphere
- **Color Philosophy**: Rich organic earths, deep mossy greens, glowing amber golds, and volcanic embers.
- **Hardware Constraint**: Strictly 4bpp (16 colors per palette), with **Color 0 dedicated as the 100% transparent key**.
- **The "No White Box" Law**:
  - No sprite shall ever render an opaque white square cardboard box around the silhouette.
  - Transparent regions in editors use the 2-tone grey checkerboard and map to GBA PPU Color 0.
  - Body whites (eyes, teeth, fangs) map to Color Index 1 or 15 to prevent transparent ghost holes.

## 2. Character & Monster Proportions
- **Protagonists**: Slightly older, proportional anime adventure aesthetic (early 20s), wearing functional outdoor expedition attire.
- **Prehistoric Pokémon**: Anatomically grounded inspired by real Mesozoic paleontology blended with iconic Pokémon elemental expressions.
