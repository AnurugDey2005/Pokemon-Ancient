## 1. Executive Summary & Core Identity

### 1.1 One-Sentence Premise
When a high-energy chronal rift fractures an Arctic paleontology expedition, two young research fellows—Leo and Maya—are stranded in the untamed prehistoric continent of Pangaea-Prime (Gondwana Basin), where they must partner with resurrected Cretaceous Pokémon, master ancient primal synergy, and stop the fanatic eco-terrorist faction Team Cataclysm from triggering a planetary extinction event.

### 1.2 Core Emotional Themes
1. **Survival & Coexistence Over Domination**: Unlike modern Pokémon leagues focused on sports entertainment and badge collecting, ancient survival requires earning the trust of untamed, apex prehistoric beasts in a world where humans are not at the top of the food chain.
2. **Comaraderie & Developing Romance**: Leo and Maya share an intimate, organic bond as partners stranded across time. Outside combat, their dynamic features genuine affection, gentle teasing, and protective loyalty; in battle, they are fiercely synchronized tactical partners.
3. **The Weight of Deep Time & Extinction**: Exploring fossilized titan skeletons, subterranean amber veins, and volcanic ruins instills awe and melancholic reverence for lost epochs.
4. **Preservation vs. Accelerated Apocalypse**: Team Cataclysm believes human civilization is a plague that ruined the Earth, seeking to awaken the primordial colossus to reset biological history, while the Ambervale Expedition seeks understanding, harmony, and safe return.

### 1.3 Target Audience & Intended Tone
- **Audience**: Lifelong Pokémon fans and ROM hack veterans who grew up with Gen 3 GBA classics and desire a rich, cinematic, maturely written adventure with modern competitive balance and quality of life.
- **Tone**: Grounded adventure with cinematic wonder (inspired by *Jurassic Park*, *Primal*, and classic anime epics). Energetic, dangerous, emotionally sincere, with zero juvenile parodies or base-ROM reskin tropes.

## 2. World & Regional Setting: The Gondwana Basin

### 2.1 Geography & Biomes
The Gondwana Basin is an isolated primordial continent sealed off from modern geological cycles:
- **Sector 1: Camp Ambervale (Starting Base)**: A rugged field research outpost nestled at the mouth of a primeval river basin surrounded by towering cycad palms, glowing amber outcrops, and fossil laboratory tents.
- **Sector 2: Primeval Riverlands (Route 1 / Route 2)**: Shallow fern-choked wetlands and mossy stone bridges where ancient amphibians and proto-reptiles nest.
- **Sector 3: The Sunken Lithosphere (Ambervale Caverns / Site Alpha)**: A subterranean network of crystalline caves rich in primordial ore, underground waterfalls, and glowing prehistoric lichens.
- **Sector 4: The Smoldering Caldera (Cretaceous Ridge)**: Volcanic badlands dominated by apex theropods and thermal geysers.
- **Sector 5: The Abyssal Trench (Primeval Sea)**: Prehistoric ocean reefs inhabited by marine reptiles and ancient ammonites.

### 2.2 Factions
1. **The Ambervale Expedition (Allies)**:
   - **Leader**: Professor Cycad (Dr. Andrea Cycad), lead paleobiologist in her late 20s. Energetic, brilliant, practical, field-dressed in safari gear and lab coat.
   - **Personnel**: Field researchers, mechanics, botanists, and medical staff stranded at Camp Ambervale.
   - **Objective**: Establish safe encampments, map the primeval ecosystems, understand ancient Pokémon biology, and repair the chronal beacon to reconnect with the future.
2. **Team Cataclysm (Antagonists)**:
   - **Leader**: Commander Vane (and Grand Director Kaelen).
   - **Philosophy**: Radical primordial accelerationism. They view modern human civilization as a decaying, corrupt blight upon nature. By using stolen chronal tech and unearthing the dormant "Apex Titan", they seek to trigger a second Cretaceous-Paleogene extinction impact to reset the Earth to its pure, primeval state.
   - **Aesthetics**: Heavy utilitarian hazard gear, oxidized obsidian armor, reinforced survival masks, and bio-tech capture harnesses.

## 3. Narrative Outline: Beginning, Middle, Climax, and Endgame

### 3.1 Act I: The Awakening (Demo V1 Scope)
- **Prologue**: The chronal collapse. No moving truck, no suburban houses. The player awakens directly on a canvas cot inside the Research Field Tent at Camp Ambervale.
- **Inciting Incident**: Siren alarms echo across Camp Ambervale. Professor Cycad's field excavation site at the northern river delta (Site Alpha) has been breached by Team Cataclysm operatives attempting to seize ancient genetic pods.
- **The Awakening of the Starters**: Rushing to Cycad's side, Leo/Maya must choose one of the three stabilized prehistoric Pokémon:
  - **Frillsprout** (Grass/Rock - The Sturdy Fern-Frill Ceratopsian)
  - **Pyroraptor** (Fire/Dark - The Cunning Ember-Clawed Hunter)
  - **Plesioling** (Water/Dragon - The Graceful Tide-Current Marine Reptile)
- **First Blood**: The player battles and defeats Cataclysm Infiltrator Jax, securing Site Alpha.
- **The Rival's Resolve**: Returning to camp, your companion (Maya if boy, Leo if girl) chooses the counter-starter and challenges you at Route 103's scenic river bluff—a battle of passionate rivalry, romantic camaraderie, and tactical growth.

### 3.2 Act II: The Primeval Expedition
- Mapping the 8 Great Biome Sanctuaries.
- Battling Ancient Biome Wardens (replacing traditional gym leaders with respected Expedition Guild Masters and Native Primeval Shamans).
- Uncovering ancient murals that tell of the "Great Extinction Comet" and the two Primordial Deities that fought to preserve life: **Aethelgard** (The Primordial Aegis) and **Cataclysmon** (The World-Ender).

### 3.3 Act III: The Cataclysm Protocol & Climax
- Team Cataclysm activates the Deep Geo-Drill at Mount Obsidian, threatening to crack the tectonic mantle.
- Leo, Maya, and Professor Cycad launch an assault on the Cataclysm Core Facility.
- Multi-battle climax alongside Maya/Leo against Commander Vane and Director Kaelen.
- Summoning and calming the Primordial Legendaries in a cinematic, multi-phase encounter.

### 3.4 Postgame: The Chronal Gateway
- Rebuilding the permanent Temporal Bridge.
- Option to explore the Untamed Cretaceous Wilds—a massive open postgame zone with high-level apex titans, hidden legendary nests, and boss rematches.

## 6. Technical & Engine Architecture Constraints

1. **Base Engine**: `pokeemerald-expansion` (GCC ARM None EABI toolchain on Ubuntu runner).
2. **ROM Size**: 32MB standard GBA cartridge boundary (`33,554,432 bytes`).
3. **Graphics Architecture**:
   - 4-bit indexed palettes (4bpp, 16 colors per palette including Color 0 transparent key).
   - Absolute prohibition against solid white box backgrounds on sprites.
   - All sprite transparent boundaries mapped to Color Index 0.
4. **Safety & Zero Antivirus Intrusion**:
   - Cloud compilation via GitHub Actions CI. Zero local native compiler execution on user's Windows machine.
   - Clean `.gba` binaries and standard zip packaging for instant mobile (ZArchiver) and PC emulator compatibility.
