# CHARACTER BIBLE: POKÉMON ANCIENT

## 1. Leo (Male Protagonist / Male Rival)
- **Age**: 21
- **Role**: Field Archaeologist & Ancient Artifact Specialist.
- **Visuals**: Messy brown hair, rugged tan field vest over black long-sleeve, tactical cargo pants, sturdy hiking boots, brown utility belt.
- **Personality**: Grounded, protective, sharp-eyed, analytical.
- **Dynamic with Maya**: Has an obvious soft spot for Maya. Easily flustered by her playful teasing, but fiercely protective of her in dangerous encounters.
- **Combat Style**: Tactical, deliberate, utilizes entry hazards, status pressure, and calculated stat boosts.

## 2. Maya (Female Protagonist / Female Rival)
- **Age**: 21
- **Role**: Field Botanist & Prehistoric Flora/Fauna Specialist.
- **Visuals**: Long raven hair tied with an olive-green bandana, active athletic frame, fitted safari tank, durable utility shorts, climbing harness, confident grin.
- **Personality**: High-energy, audacious, fiercely competitive, brilliantly intuitive.
- **Dynamic with Leo**: Enjoys teasing Leo about his serious demeanour; values his reliability above all else and harbors deep romantic affection for him.
- **Combat Style**: Aggressive, dynamic momentum, rapid pivots, high offensive pressure.

## 3. Professor Andrea Cycad
- **Age**: 28
- **Role**: Head Paleobiologist of Expedition Ambervale.
- **Visuals**: Auburn hair in a loose ponytail, forehead brass goggles, unbuttoned white lab coat over safari attire.
- **Personality**: Enthusiastic, maternal, courageous, deeply ethical. Treats ancient Pokémon as majestic living creatures rather than test subjects.

## 4. Commander Vane
- **Age**: 34
- **Role**: Tactical Commander of Team Cataclysm.
- **Visuals**: Dark scarred armor forged from volcanic obsidian, grim expression, tactical respirator collar.
- **Philosophy**: "Nature's greatest gift to Earth was the asteroid that cleared the slate. Humanity is an infection that overdue extinction must cure."
