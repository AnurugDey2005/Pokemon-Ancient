# BALANCE DESIGN: POKÉMON ANCIENT

## 1. Starter Dynamic & Battle Synergy
- **Frillsprout / Ferntops / Titanoceratops**:
  - Grass/Rock type provides unique defensive coverage against Fire and Flying threats, while possessing Solid Rock to mitigate super-effective strikes.
  - Learnset emphasizes sustain (Horn Leech, Synthesis) and heavy physical retaliation (Rock Slide, Head Smash).
- **Pyroraptor / Ignisaurus / Apex Tyrannovore**:
  - Fire/Dark type offers complete psychic immunity and explosive physical offense.
  - Fragile defense compensated by high speed and priority (Sucker Punch, Flame Charge).
- **Plesioling / Elasmostorm / Mosasurge**:
  - Water/Dragon type provides incredible elemental resistances (only weak to Dragon and Fairy).
  - High special attack and bulk (Dragon Pulse, Scald, Ice Beam).

## 2. Rival Progression & Counterplay
- Rival always picks the type-advantage starter.
- Rival AI flags (`AI_FLAG_CHECK_BAD_MOVE | AI_FLAG_TRY_TO_FAINT | AI_FLAG_CHECK_VIABILITY`) ensure tactical decision-making rather than random move selection.
