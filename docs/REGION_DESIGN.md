# REGION DESIGN: THE GONDWANA BASIN

## 1. Primeval Geography & Landmarks
- **Camp Ambervale**: Main research colony, medical tents, communication array, fossil generator.
- **Primeval Wetlands (Route 101/102)**: Giant horsetail reeds, shallow streams, dragonflies.
- **Site Alpha**: Ancient excavation quarry where starter fossils are restored.
- **Amber Bluff (Route 103)**: A majestic cliff overlooking the prehistoric river basin; site of the first battle against your partner.
- **Titan's Hollow**: Enormous fossilized skeleton spanning an entire canyon.
- **Obsidian Caldera**: Active volcanic territory harboring apex fire and rock behemoths.

## 2. Cultural & Visual Identity
- No paved concrete roads or neon billboards.
- Pathways are marked by wooden torches, ancient carved stone totems, and expedition rope bridges.
- Weather ranges from primordial rainstorms to amber sunrises and volcanic ashfalls.
